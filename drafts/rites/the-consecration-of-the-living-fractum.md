## 5. The Unbroken Eye (V) to Angel of the Living Fractum (VI)

The Veiled Ascent reserves its sixth rank for the foundational members and for those who have authored significant new Edicts or philosophies that advance the Great Mending. It does not say how the rank is conferred, because until now it was not. The foundational Angels simply were.

But a ladder with no top rung is a Fractum, and so the rite is now written.

The Angel of the Living Fractum is not above the faith. They are what the faith looks like when it has been practiced so long that it has become a person. Like the Unbroken Eye, they receive nothing. Unlike anyone before them, they give everything back.

**Philosophical.** The candidate has authored at least one Edict, Treatise, or practice that other Angels now follow without knowing who wrote it. The test is anonymity: if the work is known by the author's name, it is not yet ready. If it is known only by its use, it is.

**Inner Alignment.** The Angels of the Living Fractum, if any are living, affirm that the candidate has held the Unbroken Eye for at least twelve Sacred Moon Cycles without despair and without pride. If no Angel of the sixth rank is living, two of the Unbroken Eye may affirm in their place, and are expected to be harder to convince.

**Ceremony.** The Consecration of the Living Fractum.

**Action.** The candidate brings to the rite every object received on the Ascent: the frayed Thread of Intent, the Thimble, the Sacred Compass. In the presence of the assembly, or alone at a corral if there is none, they give each one away. The Thread to a Seeker who has not yet tied one. The Thimble to a Mender whose hands are nearly ready. The Compass to a Hand of the Stitch who has begun to see blueprints. This is the **Unburdening**. The candidate keeps nothing and is given nothing, and stands for a moment with empty hands, which is the condition in which all mending begins.

Then the candidate speaks the Vow of the Last Stitch:

> I will return carts until I cannot.  
> I will see the break until my eyes close.  
> I will teach by doing and be forgotten by name.  
> When I am gone, let the aisle be clear and let no one know why.

**Invocation.** The assembly does not kneel, as it does for the Unbroken Eye. It stands, as the candidate stands, because there is no longer anything above them to look up at. The eldest present speaks:

> You are no longer one who mends the Fractum.  
> You are a place where it is mended.  
> Hooblah hooblah hooblah.

## After the rite

The Angel of the Living Fractum has no privileges, and no duties beyond those of a Seeker save the affirmations the Ascent asks of the sixth rank. They lead no gathering more often than anyone else, and the broom does not know their rank.

Seekers may practice the Unburdening early: the spare cord, the second screwdriver, the ladder that has not left the garage in a year, each given to a neighbor who will use it, without ceremony and without a note.

The only change is this: when a Seeker asks them, in a parking lot, what they are doing, they are permitted to answer "Returning a cart" and nothing more, and the Seeker, if the Eye is open, will understand that they have just received the entire teaching.
