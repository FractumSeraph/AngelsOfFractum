This mission repairs a thread that frays in silence: the one that runs from a borrowed object back to the hand that lent it.

- **The Break:** Somewhere in your dwelling is a thing that is not yours. The drill from the neighbor. The novel from a coworker. The casserole dish that arrived full in a hard week and has sat empty in your cupboard since. The owner will not ask for it, out of politeness. You will not return it, out of a shame that grows by the week, because now it is not merely borrowed; it is *late*. The object sits in exile, and the thread between two people grows thin enough to see through.

- **The Mission:** Today, return one borrowed thing. Clean it. Charge it. If you can improve it, improve it: the blade sharpened, the tank filled, the dish returned with something in it. Hand it over with one sentence, *"Thank you, this helped,"* and no second sentence. Do not explain the delay. An excuse turns a return into a negotiation, and the lender did not ask to negotiate.

- **The Lesson:** The thing was never the point. The thread was. A drill returned after a year does not mend the year, but it proves the thread still holds weight, and a thread that holds weight can be pulled on again. The thread was never cut. Only dropped. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
