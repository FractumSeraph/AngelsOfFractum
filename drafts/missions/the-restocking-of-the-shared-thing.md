This mission takes place wherever a thing is shared and no one is assigned to it, which is to say, everywhere.

- **The Break:** The paper-towel roll with one sheet left, hanging like a flag of surrender. The soap dispenser that gasps. The printer tray with a single curled page. The coffee pot with one burnt inch at the bottom, kept warm for no one, by no one. Each of these is a Minor Breach, and each is attended by the same whispered liturgy: *someone will get it.* The someone is a Keeper you have never met, who arrives after you have gone, and who has been getting it, for everyone, for years.

- **The Mission:** Today, when you take the last of a shared thing, or the near-last, replace it. The roll goes on. The dispenser is refilled or the empty one is reported to whoever holds the key. The tray is loaded. The pot is rinsed and brewed, and if you do not know how, today is a fine day to learn, because the Keeper who knows is tired. If you truly cannot replace the thing, leave a note where the next pilgrim will see it, so that they are not the third person to discover it empty.

- **The Invocation:** Whisper the sacred threefold phrase over the fresh roll, the full tray, the pot as it begins to drip. No one will know it was you. The next person to reach for the thing will simply find it there, which is what the world feels like when it is mended. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
