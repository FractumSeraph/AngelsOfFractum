This mission is one of the three Foundational Mending Missions (Cart, Pause, Minor Breach) and the one most often failed, because it must be performed inside a single second.

- **The Break:** Between the moment a Seeker notices a slight and the moment they respond to it, there is a gap so small that most people do not know it exists. The car that cuts in. The cashier who is slow. The curt reply to a kind message. The Fractum lives in that gap, and it moves fast: before you have decided anything, your face has already decided, your horn has already decided, your thumb has already typed "k." Reflex is the Fractum's native speed.

- **The Mission:** Today, when something small goes wrong at the hands of another person, take one full breath before you do anything. In that breath, perform the Immersion of Self: the driver who cut in may be late for a hospital. The cashier may be on hour nine. The curt reply may have been typed one-handed, in a hallway, by someone who is not fine. You are not required to know. You are required to allow that you do not. Then respond as you would to that person, not to the slight.

- **The Invocation:** The breath is the whole invocation. If you need words, say the first "Hooblah" on the inhale and the last two on the exhale, silently, and you will find that the second has passed and the reflex with it. The Fractum cannot survive one breath. It never could.
