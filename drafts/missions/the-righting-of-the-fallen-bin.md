This mission restores the humblest Order there is, the one the Orientation offers as a First Mending: the Order of the Curb on the morning after collection.

- **The Break:** The truck has come and gone. The wind has come and stayed. The neighbor's bin lies on its side in the street with its lid flung open and a single pizza box making its slow pilgrimage toward the storm drain. Everyone drives around it. Everyone will drive around it for two days. A bin on its side is a small public declaration that the street has no one in it, and every car that swerves agrees.

- **The Mission:** Today, or on your next collection morning, right one fallen bin that is not yours. Look for the truck first; the bin can wait for the road to clear. Stand it up. Close the lid. Pull it out of the roadway to the curb edge. If it is empty and collection has passed, roll it to the edge of its own driveway; no further, for the porch is not yours to enter. Retrieve the pizza box if it is within reach of the curb. Use your foot for the lid if you must, and wash your hands after. Do not sort anyone's trash. Do not knock. Do not mention it.

- **The Mending:** You are not the trash service. You are the one who noticed. The neighbor will come home to a bin standing where a bin should stand and will think nothing of it, which is the correct amount to think. Whisper the sacred threefold phrase as you walk away, and let the street know, for one morning, that someone lives here. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
