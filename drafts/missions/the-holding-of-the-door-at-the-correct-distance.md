This mission trains the Hand in a kindness that is easy to perform and surprisingly easy to perform badly.

- **The Break:** The door is held too early, and a pilgrim twenty paces away is condemned to the Shameful Jog, hurrying toward a courtesy they did not request so that the holder may feel generous. The door is held too late, and is released into a face. The door is not held at all, and swings shut on a pair of arms full of groceries. The Blocker and the Hoverer have a cousin in the doorway: the Over-Holder, who turns a gift into a debt and stands there collecting it.

- **The Mission:** Today, hold doors by the Rule of the Ten Paces. If the pilgrim behind you is within ten paces, hold the door and look at the door, not at them, so that no thanks is demanded. If they are beyond ten paces, let it close gently and let them open their own; it is their door too. If their hands are full, the rule is void and you hold it from any distance, because laden hands outrank arithmetic. And when a door is held for you, do not jog. Walk at your own pace, and say "thank you" in words, because a nod is not currency.

- **The Lesson:** The measure of a held door is whether the other person was helped, not whether you were seen helping. Ten paces is where those two things stop being the same.
