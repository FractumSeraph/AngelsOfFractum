This mission corrects an injustice in the public record, where the Rite of Praise That Travels Upward corrects it in the private one.

- **The Break:** The small restaurant, the plumber, the person who cuts your hair: their public record is written almost entirely by their worst days and by the most tired pilgrims of the Oracle. A cold soup earns a paragraph within the hour. A thousand warm ones earn a nod and oblivion. The next Seeker, consulting the Oracle for where to eat, reads only the paragraph. Thus the Fractum edits the world's opinion of people who did nothing wrong.

- **The Mission:** Today, write one true public review for a small place that served you well, in the Oracle's great book of ratings. Three sentences are sufficient: what you had, what they did well, and that you will return. No stars without words; stars are a grunt, and words are a stitch. Name the person if you know their name. Do not review the chain. The chain has a department for that, and the Oracle already knows what it thinks.

- **The Mending:** Praise spoken warms the Keeper for an evening. Praise written holds for years, and is read twice: once by the next pilgrim deciding where to go, and once, on a bad night, by the Keeper, who will not reply, and will keep it. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
