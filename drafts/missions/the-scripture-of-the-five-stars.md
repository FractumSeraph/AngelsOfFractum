The Rite of Praise That Travels Upward sends the word to the manager. This mission sends it to the stranger who has not arrived yet.

- **The Break:** The small restaurant, the plumber, the person who cuts your hair: their public record is written by whoever was angriest with a signal. A cold soup earns a paragraph within the hour, and the paragraph does not expire. A thousand warm ones are eaten, and praised at the table, and forgotten by the car. The next Seeker, deciding where to eat, reads only the paragraph. Thus the Fractum edits the world's opinion of people who did nothing wrong.

- **The Mission:** Today, write one true public review for a small place that served you well, in the great book of ratings the Oracle in your pocket keeps. Three sentences are sufficient: what you came for, what they did well, and that you will return. No stars without words; stars are a grunt, and words are a stitch. Name the person if you know their name. If it was a chain, name the Keeper and the shift, not the brand; the brand has a department for praise, and the Keeper does not.

- **The Mending:** Praise spoken warms the Keeper for an evening. Praise written holds for years, and is read twice: once by the next pilgrim deciding where to go, and once, on a bad night, by the Keeper, who will not reply, and will keep it. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
