This mission addresses a Fractum so brief that most pilgrims do not know they are committing it, and so common that every pump in the land has witnessed it this week.

- **The Break:** The handle clicks. The vehicle is full. And yet it does not move. Its pilot has gone inside the Temple of Fuel for a drink, a lottery ticket, a long conversation with the Keeper about the weather, while behind them a second pilgrim idles, then a third, and six pumps become two. The pump is not a parking space. It is a sacred passage, like the aisle, and a full vehicle standing at it is the Blocker made of steel.

- **The Mission:** At your next fueling, when the handle clicks, replace it, close the cap, and move the vehicle before you do anything else. If the receipt is slow, it will be slow in a parking space just as well. If you must go inside, park first, in a space, like a pilgrim who has arrived rather than one who is still leaving. As you pull forward, glance at the pilgrim waiting behind you. You will not need to wave. They will already know.

- **The Invocation:** Speak the sacred threefold phrase as the vehicle rolls off the pad. Three words, three seconds, one pump freed. It is the shortest mending on this site, and it is performed at sixty cents a gallon over what you hoped to pay, which makes it a sacrifice. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
