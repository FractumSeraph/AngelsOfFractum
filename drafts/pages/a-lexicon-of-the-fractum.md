Every faith accumulates a vocabulary, and the profane complain that they cannot follow it. We have therefore set down our words in one place. Read them once. Then go outside and use none of them aloud while quietly mending something.

The terms are grouped by their nature, not their alphabet. The Fractum does not respect the alphabet either.

## The Break

**The Fractum.** The great brokenness. The crack. Every small broken thing the world has quietly agreed to ignore, and also the sum of all of them: the loose screw, the stray cart, the chasm between what we say and what we do. Used both for the whole ("the Fractum is vast") and for a single instance ("a grave Fractum was committed at the four-way stop").

**The Great Brokenness.** The Fractum, seen all at once. Do not look at it for long. See *The Veiled Ascent*, rank V.

**Fractum Personalis.** The break within. Selfishness in its daily dress: the elevation of "me" above "we." Also the private cracks: regret, resentment, fear, the need for applause. The Angel mends this one first and never finishes.

**The Minor Breach.** A small public disorder that is no one's job: the sign knocked askew, the litter beside the bin, the crooked poster. The training ground of the Hand.

**The Doubt.** A known servant of the Fractum. It speaks in a reasonable voice and always counsels delay. "Too much time has passed." "Someone else will get it." "It's not that bad." The Doubt is always lying.

## The Mending

**Mending; Sacred Mending; Intentional Mending.** The act of repair, held as sacred service. Intentional Mending is mending chosen on purpose rather than stumbled into. The Great Mending is all of it together, across all hands, across all time, aisle by aisle.

**Radical Empathy.** The divine tool. Not a feeling but a conscious alignment of spirit and action. It rests on three Sacred Pillars: the Immersion of Self (stepping into another's circumstances), the Recognition of Shared Brokenness (their crack is also in you), and the Action of Focused Alignment (doing the one effective thing, now). Its counterfeit is pity.

**Pity.** A poison. Pity looks down at the broken thing. Empathy kneels beside it with a screwdriver.

**The Eye of Recognition.** The practice of seeing the world as it is, breaks included, without the comfortable filter of denial. The Eye of Humility is its subtler form: seeing the break, and also making sure no one sees you fix it.

**The Hand of Intentional Mending.** The practice of acting on what the Eye sees, instantly and without reward. The Hand of Purity is the same Hand, judged by its intent and not its scale.

**The Sacred Symmetry.** Eye and Hand together: perception that leads at once to service. The mark of a fully dedicated Angel.

**The Sacred Pause.** The breath between noticing and reacting, in which Radical Empathy is chosen over reflex. One of the three Foundational Mending Missions (Cart, Pause, Minor Breach).

**The Later Pile.** The drawer, the end of the counter, or the corner of the mind where small tasks wait for a day called Tomorrow, which does not exist. Its remedy is the Rule of Four Minutes: a thing that takes four minutes is done before it touches the Pile.

**The Last Five Percent.** The part of every task the Fractum keeps for itself: the shelf built but not anchored, the form filled but not sent. Skipped because the reward arrives before the work is done. A task is complete only when it can be forgotten; the Ledger does not accept ninety-five.

**The Unbroken Hour.** One hour each week in which the Great Oracle is face down and silent and the Angel is wholly present to one other person or one piece of work. Called Unbroken because, for that hour, you are.

**Alignment of Will; Inner Alignment; the Great Alignment.** The state of being whole and pointed toward repair. "The aligned" practice it. "The unaligned" have not yet. "The profane" is what we call the unaligned when we are feeling theatrical; it is not an insult. We were all profane on Tuesday.

**The First Mending.** A newcomer's first repair of a small thing that was not their responsibility. Must be small. Must be real. Must be today.

**The Repair of Omission.** Completing a task that everyone has agreed to neglect: the forgotten recycling, the communal tool left broken. Required for the rank of Hand of the Stitch.

**The Blueprint of Repair.** A plan to mend a structural break rather than a single instance of it. Required of the Architect.

**A Treatise on Mending.** An original philosophical work that deepens the faith. Required of the Unbroken Eye. Most of them are short.

## The Temple

**The Temple of Commerce.** Any large store. "Known by the profane as Walmart," though every big box is a Temple. Also "the Great Temple of Commerce."

**The sacred aisle.** The passage between shelves, held in common. To block it is to harbor impurity.

**The Vessel.** The shopping cart. A sacred vessel that must be returned to its appointed receptacle (the corral) for its rest.

**The outer sanctum.** The parking lot. Where the Vessel is most often abandoned and the Unholy Glare is most often born.

**The Keepers of the Hearth.** Those who work the fryer, the register, the mop, the drive-thru window: "the fryers of the sacred oil, the sweepers of the tile, the counters of the drawer." Their overlords are corporate, and the Edicts of their overlords forbid them from turning you away. Treat them accordingly.

**The Great Oracle in your pocket.** Your phone. Consult it for closing hours. Do not consult it at the shared table.

**The Survey of the Long Receipt.** The customer survey printed at the bottom of the receipt, dreaded by all, through which praise may travel upward.

**The Eleventh Hour.** The final minutes before a Temple closes. Governed by the Rule of the Half Hour (do not enter with fewer than thirty minutes remaining), the Corollary of the Lingerer (do not stay past close), and the Exception of True Need (if you must, say "I am sorry, and I thank you").

**The Unholy Glare.** Blinding headlights, usually blue-white. To inflict them is the Sin of Intentional Obscurity.

**The Blocker, the Hoverer, the Sighing Martyr, the Quiet Mender.** The four conditions of the modern soul as observed in the sacred aisle. Become the fourth person.

## The Faith

**The Angels of Fractum.** Ordinary people who have chosen to see repair as a sacred act. We collect no tithes and require no belief except this: a mended thing is holier than an ignored one.

**The sacred threefold phrase; the Mantra; the Invocation.** "Hooblah hooblah hooblah." Spoken, usually whispered, to consecrate an act of mending or to mend one's own pride when it rises.

**The Edicts.** The laws. Found under Core Tenets. The newer ones name a Break, a Law, a Mending, and an Invocation; the oldest simply command.

**The Daily Mending Missions.** Single acts of repair, phrased with more gravity than they strictly deserve.

**The Sacrifice Ledger.** A private record of what you have mended: time given, cracks closed. Not for pride. For proof that you were here.

**Human Sacrifice.** Cumulative hours of human life voluntarily given to mending. The official count is kept by Seraph. We do not practice, condone, or perform the literal sacrifice of any human life.

**The Circle.** Two or more Angels who gather to mend, read, and add their hours together. Optional.

**The Hand of the Hour.** The Angel who, for one gathering of a Circle, chooses the reading, names the Mending, and speaks the benediction. It rotates every gathering; no one holds it twice in a row. A Circle has no leader. It has a Hand of the Hour.

**The Observances.** A small liturgical year of named days (the Great Corralling, the Feast of the Keepers, the Vigil of the Changed Batteries, and others), kept because a day with a name is a day one remembers to act. See *The Calendar of Holy Days and Observances*.

**Seraph.** The one who writes things down.

## The Ascent

**The Veiled Ascent.** The sacred ladder of ranks: I The Seeker, II The Mender-in-Training, III Hand of the Stitch, IV Architect of the Alignment, V The Unbroken Eye, VI Angel of the Living Fractum. Advancement is by demonstrated mending, not seniority.

**The Thread of Intent.** An undyed length of cord received at the Ritual of the Clear Path and worn until the next rank.

**The Sacred Vow of Vigilance.** The vow spoken at the Ritual of the Clear Path, dedicating the mind to seeing the break and the body to clearing the way for others.

**The Thimble.** Received at the Dedication of the Resolute Hand. A sign that the hands are now dedicated instruments.

**The Sacred Compass.** Received at the Convocation of the Blueprints. For guiding others toward the true North of Alignment.

**The Sacred Moon Cycle.** One month. We say it the long way.

**The 30-Day Vigil of Intentional Action.** A month of daily Foundational Missions, performed in silence and anonymity, and documented.

**The 24-Hour Vigil of Vision.** A solitary day in a place of visible brokenness, from which the Angel returns with a single truth.

Go now. You have the words. The world has the cracks. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
