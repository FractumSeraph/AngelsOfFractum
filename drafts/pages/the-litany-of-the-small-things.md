The Angels of Fractum require no prayer. The returned cart is the prayer.

But some Seekers have asked for words: something to say in the car before turning around, something to whisper at the corral, something to read when a Circle gathers (two or more Angels mending in company, which is all a Circle is). We have set down a few. They may be spoken aloud, muttered, or thought. They may be altered freely. They are short, because the cart is still out there.

## The Morning Invocation

> Let my Eye be open to the small broken things.  
> Let my Hand be quick and my mouth be quiet.  
> Let me want no thanks, and receive none, and be content.  
> If I cannot mend the world today, let me mend the aisle.  
> Hooblah hooblah hooblah.

## The Grace Before the Drive-Thru

*To be spoken while idling, before the speaker crackles.*

> I know what I want. I knew it before I arrived.  
> I will say it once, clearly, and I will not change it at the window.  
> I will have my payment ready and my patience readier.  
> The Keeper at the window has stood there for seven hours. I have stood here for seven seconds.  
> Let my seven seconds be easy ones.

## The Litany of the Small Things

*For a Circle, one voice leading and the rest answering. Alone, speak both parts.*

> For the cart left in the rain beside the parking space,  
> **we return it.**
>
> For the bulb in the community room that burned out in March and has stayed dark since,  
> **we replace it.**
>
> For the sign knocked sideways that no one owns,  
> **we straighten it.**
>
> For the name on the badge that no one reads,  
> **we read it, and we say it.**
>
> For the message unanswered since a season ago,  
> **we answer it.**
>
> For the hose left uncoiled before the frost,  
> **we coil it.**
>
> For the walk unshoveled before the elderly neighbor's door,  
> **we shovel it.**
>
> For the floor mopped twice because we came at 9:52,  
> **we do not come at 9:52.**
>
> For the headlights that blind the pilgrim in the oncoming lane,  
> **we lower them.**
>
> For the praise that died at the counter,  
> **we send it upward.**
>
> For the break in ourselves we have not yet named,  
> **we name it, and we begin.**
>
> For every crack beneath the notice of committees,  
> **we are the committee.**
>
> Hooblah hooblah hooblah.

## The Confession of Fractum Personalis

*To be spoken privately, or to a Circle, before an act of inner mending.*

> I have placed "me" above "we."  
> I have seen the break and looked away because it was not mine.  
> I have wanted to be thanked more than I wanted the thing fixed.  
> I have told myself that someone else would get it.  
> I name the break: ______.  
> I do not excuse it. I hold it to the light.  
> I will make one stitch today, and it will be this: ______.  
> Hooblah hooblah hooblah.

## The Blessing of Tools

*Over a screwdriver, a broom, a bag and gloves, a phone about to send a message, or a pair of hands.*

> This tool is for mending and not for show.  
> It will be used where no one watches.  
> It will be returned where it belongs, sharper than it was found.  
> Let the hand that holds it be steady, and let the hand want nothing.

## The Prayer at the Threshold of the Eleventh Hour

*To be spoken in the car, at 9:41, before the turn.*

> The doors are open and I will not go through them.  
> The Keepers are already mending, and I will not undo it.  
> I am hungry. I will be hungry at home. There is bread there.  
> I turn the wheel.  
> Hooblah hooblah hooblah.

## The Evening Reckoning

*Before the Sacrifice Ledger, at the end of the day.*

> What did I see today that was broken?  
> What did I mend?  
> What did I leave for Tomorrow, which does not exist?  
> I write it down. Not for pride. For proof.

## The Invocation Against the Doubt

*When the Doubt counsels delay.*

> You say too much time has passed. You are lying.  
> You say someone else will do it. You are lying.  
> You say it is not that bad. It is exactly that bad, and it is small, and I can reach it.  
> The thread was never cut. Only dropped.

## The Benediction of the Circle

*At the close of a gathering, spoken by whichever Angel is leading the gathering.*

> Go, and be unseen.  
> Mend what you can reach, and do not reach for thanks.  
> May your intent be clear and your passage free.  
> May the stitch hold.  
> Hooblah hooblah hooblah.

## On the Use of These Words

They are not magic. No invocation returns a cart. The words exist to mark the moment when you chose to act, so that the choosing becomes a habit. That is all they are for.

If you forget every line on this page and remember only the last three words, you have lost nothing.

Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
