We have robes. We have Edicts. We have a rank called the Unbroken Eye and a running count of Human Sacrifice. We are aware of how this looks.

A faith that borrows the furniture of a cult owes the world a plain statement, in plain words, of what it will never do. This is that statement. Hold us to it.

## We do not want your money

Not now, not later, not at a higher rank. There is no tithe, no dues, no suggested donation, no store. If something on this site ever asks you to pay, the site has been broken, and you should treat it as a Minor Breach and tell us.

If you have money to give, give it to the food pantry, the library, the shelter. Write it in your Ledger as mending. It is.

## We do not want you alone

Cults isolate. We do the opposite: half of our missions are instructions to reach outward. Text the friend you have drifted from. Learn the cashier's name. Shovel the neighbor's walk. An Angel who has grown distant from family in the name of the Fractum has misread every page of this site, and we would like to speak with them, gently, with a screwdriver.

If anyone, in our name, tells you to cut off a person who loves you, they are not one of us.

## We have no secret teachings

Everything we believe is on this website, which is free, which anyone may read, and which no rank unlocks. There is no inner doctrine revealed at rank IV. The Architect knows exactly what the Seeker knows; the Architect has simply returned more carts.

## We have no masters

Seraph writes things down. Seraph has no authority over you and does not want any. The ranks of the Veiled Ascent describe practice, not power: an Angel of the Living Fractum cannot tell a Seeker what to do, only show them where the corral is.

No Angel may be commanded. No Circle may be ruled. If a Circle forms around a person rather than around the work, it has become something else, and you may leave it without ceremony.

## We do not mock your faith

We borrow the language of liturgy because it is beautiful and because it makes people laugh while they do good things. We do not borrow it to wound. The Angels of Fractum take no position on God, gods, the afterlife, or the proper day of rest. Our Edicts concern parking lots.

If you pray, pray. If you do not, do not. Then return the cart. The gate does not ask whose hands latched it.

## We do not ask you to suffer

The work of mending is sometimes thankless and often tiring, and we say so. But the Angel who is exhausted, resentful, or unwell has become the broken vessel trying to fill the ocean. Rest is maintenance. Set down the tool. An Angel on the floor mends nothing.

If the practice ever makes you feel smaller, stop practicing and tell someone you trust.

## We do not want you to confront anyone

The Hand mends. It does not scold. We do not want you to lecture the Blocker, honk at the Glare, or correct the stranger in the Eleventh Hour. Nearly every mission on this site can be completed in silence, and the best of them are. If an act of mending requires you to raise your voice at a stranger, it is not the act we meant.

## We do not keep records of you

The Sacrifice Ledger is yours. We never see it. There is no roster of Angels, no list of ranks, no map of Circles. If you stop practicing tomorrow, no one will call.

## What we are

Ordinary people who have agreed to see the small broken things and to fix the ones within reach. That is the entire organization. It has no address.

The robes are a bit. The kindness is not.

Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
