The Temple of Commerce has its Keepers of the Hearth, paid and badged. The place where you work has a hearth also, and no one is paid to keep it, which is why its keeping falls on everyone and lands on one.

## The Break

Every workplace has a shared room, and every shared room has a small Fractum running in it at all hours. The microwave, its door open, its interior a record of every lunch since spring. The mug in the sink that has been "soaking" since Monday. The cold box, in which a container with no name and no date has outlasted two of the people who might have owned it. The printer, jammed, with four people's jobs queued behind it and a fifth person walking away from it pressing cancel.

The liturgy of this Fractum is a single word, and everyone in the building has said it: *later.*

## The Law

**The Rule of the Last Cup.** Whoever takes the last cup brews the next pot. Not later. Now, while the pot is in your hand.

**The Rule of the Sink.** Your dish is washed by your hands on the day you used it. "Soaking" is not a state of matter; it is a prayer that someone else will do it. If there is a dishwasher, the dish goes in it, and if the dishwasher is clean, you shall empty it first, which is the Repair of Omission, the mending of a thing no one broke and no one did.

**The Rule of the Cold Box.** Nothing nameless. Nothing dateless. Nothing older than a week. When the Friday purge comes, whoever performs it should find your container already gone, because you took it home Thursday. The cold box is not storage. It is a loan.

**The Corollary of the Jammed Machine.** The pilgrim who finds the printer jammed shall clear it or report it. Walking away pressing cancel, so that the next pilgrim finds the jam and your job both, is a double Fractum. If the machine is beyond you, the note on it reads "Jammed, reported, sorry," and your name, so that the next pilgrim knows help is coming.

## The Mending

- Learn the pot. Learn the printer. Ten minutes of instruction will serve you for years, and will make you the one who knows, which is the only office the break room has.
- Once a Sacred Moon Cycle, open the cold box and remove only what is yours. What is not yours is not yours to purge. If the Temple has appointed you, post the date a week ahead where every pilgrim will see it, and on the day, spare nothing and say the phrase.
- When the pot is fresh and you did not brew it, find out who did, and say so, to them, in one sentence, and move on before they can wave it off.

## The Invocation

As the fresh pot begins to drip, as the clean mug goes in the rack, as the jam clears and the queue lurches back to life, speak the phrase. The hearth is kept. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
