The Temple of Commerce has its Keepers of the Hearth. The place where you work has a hearth also, and its Keepers are the people you sit beside, who did not sign up to be Keepers, and who are keeping it anyway.

## The Break

Every workplace has a shared room, and every shared room has a small Fractum running in it at all hours. The coffee pot with one burnt inch left in it, kept warm for no one by someone who did not want to be the one to brew. The mug in the sink that has been "soaking" since Tuesday. The cold box, in which a container with no name and no date has achieved a kind of sentience. The printer, jammed, with four people's jobs queued behind it and a fifth person walking away from it pressing cancel.

The liturgy of this Fractum is a single sentence, and everyone in the building has said it: *someone will get it.*

## The Law

**The Rule of the Last Cup.** Whoever takes the last cup brews the next pot. Not later. Now, while the pot is in your hand. If you do not know how, the Keeper who knows is at their desk, tired, and will teach you in ninety seconds, and will remember that you asked.

**The Rule of the Sink.** Your dish is washed by your hands on the day you used it. "Soaking" is not a state of matter; it is a prayer that someone else will do it. If there is a dishwasher, the dish goes in it, and if the dishwasher is clean, you shall empty it first, which is the Repair of Omission in its most domestic form.

**The Rule of the Cold Box.** Nothing nameless. Nothing dateless. Nothing older than a week. When the Friday purge comes, the Keeper who performs it should find your container already gone, because you took it home Thursday. The cold box is not storage. It is a loan.

**The Corollary of the Jammed Machine.** The pilgrim who finds the printer jammed shall clear it or report it. Walking away pressing cancel, so that the next pilgrim finds the jam and your job both, is a double Fractum. If the machine is beyond you, the note on it reads "Jammed, reported, sorry," and your name, so that the next pilgrim knows the Keeper is coming.

**The Rule of the Last Sheet.** The last of a shared thing (the paper, the towels, the sugar, the pens that are not anyone's) summons its own replacement. See *The Restocking of the Shared Thing*. The Angel who takes the last and walks away has simply moved the empty onto someone else's desk.

## The Mending

- Learn the pot. Learn the printer. Ten minutes of instruction will serve you for years, and will make you a Keeper, which is the highest rank the break room offers.
- Once a Sacred Moon Cycle, open the cold box and remove only what is yours. If you remove what is not yours, you have become the Keeper, and you may not want that; but if you do, do it in silence and say the phrase.
- When the pot is fresh and you did not brew it, find out who did, and say so. The Rite of Praise That Travels Upward works in the hallway too.

## The Invocation

As the fresh pot begins to drip, as the clean mug goes in the rack, as the jam clears and the queue lurches back to life, speak the phrase. The hearth is kept. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
