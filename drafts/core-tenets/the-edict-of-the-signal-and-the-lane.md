The Edict of the Unholy Glare governs what your vehicle says to strangers with its lamps. It says other things too, and most of them are rude.

## The Break

A vehicle cannot speak. It has, instead, a small lever that announces intention, a lane reserved for a single purpose, and a few shared customs at the places where roads meet. These are the whole language of the road, and the Fractum has nearly erased it.

The signal is given mid-turn, which is not a warning but a confession. The left lane is occupied, at a stately pace, by a pilgrim with nowhere to be, while behind them the Sighing Martyrs gather. The merge is treated as a contest of wills. And at the four-way stop, two pilgrims wave each other forward for so long that a third arrives, stops, and is waved at too, until the intersection is a small silent church in which no one will go first.

## The Law

**The Rule of the Signal.** The signal is given *before* the act, in time for the pilgrim behind to understand it. A signal given as the wheel turns is a receipt, not a notice. You shall signal every turn and every lane change, including the ones where "no one is there," because someone is always there, and because the hand must be trained in private for what it does in public.

**The Rule of the Passing Lane.** The left lane is for passing. To dwell in it is to become the Hoverer at sixty miles an hour, sealing off the sacred passage while contemplating the horizon. When you have passed, you shall return to the right. If a vehicle appears behind you in the left lane, you have already lingered too long.

**The Corollary of the Gap.** When a pilgrim signals to enter your lane, you shall make room for one. One, not the whole line; an Angel is generous, not a traffic signal. And when you are let in, you shall raise a hand, because a gap given and unacknowledged is a thread dropped.

**The Rule of the Four-Way.** First to stop is first to go. When it is your turn, you shall go, promptly and without ceremony. The duel of courtesy, in which two pilgrims wave until the light fails, is not kindness. It is two people each insisting on being the humble one, which is Fractum Personalis wearing a very polite hat. Go when it is yours. Yield when it is theirs. The road is mended by clarity, not by deference.

## The Mending

- For one week, signal in empty parking lots, at three in the morning, on your own street. When the hand does it without the mind, you are ready.
- When you notice you have been in the left lane for a mile without passing anything, move right and say the phrase. The pilgrim behind you will never know they were about to become a Martyr.
- At the four-way, count: stop, look, go. Three beats. The Sacred Pause is one breath, and a breath is enough.

## The Invocation

As the signal ticks before the turn, let it be the metronome of your alignment. Tick, tick, tick: Hooblah, hooblah, hooblah.
