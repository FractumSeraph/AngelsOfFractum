Where machines are shared, a pilgrim's laundry is not only their laundry. It is a claim upon a sacred passage, and the length of the claim is the whole question.

## The Break

The laundromat and the shared basement are Temples of a humbler kind, and their Fractum is time. A load finishes, and its pilgrim is elsewhere: at the Temple of Commerce, at home, asleep. The machine sits full and silent. A second pilgrim arrives with a basket and finds every washer occupied and half of them finished. They wait. They consider. They do not know the law, and so they do not know whether they are permitted to move a stranger's wet clothes, and so the Fractum holds the whole room hostage to the absence of one person.

Then there is the lint trap, which is a covenant written in felt. The pilgrim who does not clear it leaves the next pilgrim a dryer that takes twice as long and a small fire that has not happened yet.

## The Law

**The Rule of the Timer.** When your load begins, you shall set the Oracle to summon you two minutes before it ends, and you shall be standing before the machine when it stops. If you cannot be, you shall not start the load. The machine is lent, not rented.

**The Rule of the Trap.** You shall clear the lint trap before your load and after it. Before, because the pilgrim ahead of you may not have known the law. After, because now you do.

**The Rule of the Finished Load.** A load that has sat finished for a quarter of an hour may be moved by the waiting pilgrim: lifted, gently, in its entirety, and placed on the clean folding surface or in the owner's basket if one is there. The Angel moving another's garments shall handle them as relics: no sorting, no comment, no photograph, no single sock left in the drum. This is not theft. It is the Clearing of the Path, performed with the arms.

**The Corollary of the Dryer.** A finished dryer load may be moved in the same manner, but the Angel shall not fold it, because folding is intimacy, and the stranger did not ask for intimacy. Fold only your own.

**The Corollary of the Surface.** The folding table is for folding. It is not for the basket, the backpack, the Oracle, or the pilgrim sitting on it. Two pilgrims folding is the table's purpose, and the Angel who sees a second pilgrim approach shall gather their own work to one end.

## The Mending

- Carry a bag for your lint if the bin is far. The trap, cleared and left on top of the dryer, is a Fractum relocated, not mended.
- If you must move a stranger's load, leave a note if you have paper: "Your load was done; moved it here at 4:10. Nothing touched." A note turns a liberty into a courtesy.
- When you find the trap already cleared and the drum already empty, someone before you kept the law. Say the phrase for them. They will never know, which is how they wanted it.

## The Invocation

When the lint comes away in one grey sheet and the trap slides home clean, speak the sacred threefold phrase. It is a small thing. The fire that does not happen is not. Hooblah hooblah hooblah.
