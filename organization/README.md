# Formation packet: Church of the Angels of Fractum

Draft documents for forming an Arkansas nonprofit religious corporation and seeking IRS 501(c)(3) recognition. **Every document is a draft for review by an Arkansas attorney and is not legal advice.** Statutes, fees, forms, and deadlines were researched on November 4, 2026 against primary sources where possible; anything the research could not confirm is marked `[VERIFY]` in the text. Fees and forms change; confirm them on the Arkansas Secretary of State, DFA, and IRS sites before filing.

## The two decisions the packet turns on

1. **Church, or religious organization that is not (yet) a church.** The risk memo recommends incorporating now, starting a Fort Smith Circle on a published schedule now, fixing the website, and then applying as a 501(c)(3) religious organization (Option B), requesting church classification later once a congregation record exists. Some documents were drafted assuming the church posture from day one; where they differ, the risk memo's recommendation controls and the document says so. Counsel should settle this before anything is adopted.
2. **What the website says about itself.** The site's self-descriptions as a cult, a bit, and a joke are the single largest obstacle to IRS recognition. The risk memo lists each line and offers replacement wording that keeps the humor.

## Not in this packet

- Names of directors, officers, registered agent, and the incorporator (placeholders are listed at the end of each document).
- Local Fort Smith / Sebastian County zoning or occupancy questions for a place of worship.
- Insurance quotes. The roadmap says what an insurer will ask.
- Any filing. Nothing here has been submitted anywhere.
